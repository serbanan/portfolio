CHIPS Book

2024 · Photography by Andrei Serban

CHIPS is a photography book project by Andrei Serban, exhibited and launched at 2/3 Galeria in 2024.

CHIPS received LDA Best Book Design 2025 and was nominated for Best Book Design from all over the world by Stiftung Buchkunst in 2026.
